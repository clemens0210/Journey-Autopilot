# Footage library

24 clips from Pexels, 4 per narrative beat. All 1080p H.264, free for commercial
use, no attribution required (crediting the photographer is still good practice).

Full 53-clip shortlist with previews: see the footage shortlist artifact.
Manifest with every link tier: `footage-manifest.json` (scratchpad).

**Framing note:** two clips are DCI 2K (2048x1080), not 1920x1080 —
`04-replan/8134590` and `04-replan/9520980`. They need a ~6% crop or scale to
sit in a 1920 composition.

## What isn't here

Pexels has no DB delay board, no "Verspätung" display, and no branded ICE
interior — that footage doesn't exist in free stock. The delay beat above is
carried by generic boards, clocks and crowd pressure. A literal DB delay screen
needs to be mocked up in HyperFrames.

The bus / replacement-transport search returned nothing usable (all drab, dated
or gritty), so 04 covers the alternative with a car interior instead.
